# Review and learning guide

Use this guide to review Chapter I and provide concrete user criteria for future updates.

## Suggested playtest

1. Open the game and move around with WASD or the arrow keys.
2. Find Elder Rowan and interact with Space or Enter.
3. Walk around to encounter a Hollow Wolf.
4. Try each battle command: Fight, Magic, Item, and Run.
5. Return to the ancient well and interact after meeting Rowan.
6. Save, refresh the page, and check whether your progress is retained.

## Learning prompts

- Find the canvas drawing function and identify how the player and village are rendered.
- Find the keyboard event handlers and trace how movement is applied.
- Follow a combat action through enemy damage, enemy turn, and battle completion.
- Find how game state is stored and restored from local storage.
- Suggest one change to the battle balance and describe how you would verify it.

## Initial review checklist

- [ ] Controls and objectives are understandable without outside instructions.
- [ ] Dialogue and menu text remain readable at browser zoom and on narrow screens.
- [ ] All battle actions produce clear feedback.
- [ ] HP/MP and inventory remain valid after victory, defeat, and reload.
- [ ] Quest progression can be completed in the intended order.
- [ ] Keyboard focus and button interaction are usable.
- [ ] A tester's browser/device and reproduction steps are captured with each issue.
